Privacy Policy – DokanHisab

Last updated: November 13, 2026

DokanHisab is an offline shop debt management application.

Information stored

The app allows users to store customer names, debt records, payments and transaction history locally on their device.

Data sharing

The app does not send this information to our servers. Users may choose to export and share their backup files themselves.

Data security

App data is stored locally on the device. Users are responsible for protecting their device and backup files.

Children's privacy

DokanHisab is a business management tool and is not designed for children.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the app developer through the Google Play Store listing.
